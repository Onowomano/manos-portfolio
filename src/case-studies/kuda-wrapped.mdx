At the end of the year, people tend to review how their year went. Kuda customers are no exceptions.  
The end-of-year spending report, **#KudaWrapped**, was introduced in 2019 but hadn’t been consistent.

In 2023, we decided to bring it back, but we had a short timeline to implement it because we couldn’t get all the data early enough.  
We had **one week for design** and the remaining **three weeks for development**.

---

## Data Points

We worked with the following data points:

- Account balance  
- Deposits and transfers  
- Card transactions  
- Cardless transactions  
- Airtime & bill payments  
- Savings  
- Overdraft  
- Investment  

---

> **Goals**
> 
> - Show customers how they spent their money on Kuda  
> - Increase brand awareness  
> - Onboard new customers and reactivate old customers  

---

## Problem

- A very tight timeline to deliver quickly and efficiently  
- The project was voluntary, so we worked on it alongside other assignments  
- More data points than previous years due to additional features and improved data tracking  

---

## Solution

Since this was a dynamic website, we first agreed on a theme. We retained a similar visual style to previous years but used different layouts for the various sections. Each section was split into categories, and multiple categories were assigned to different team members.

We used the existing flow, which required customers to enter their account numbers so a link to their report could be sent to the email associated with their Kuda account. This ensured no one else could access their financial information.

![User Flow for Requesting Report](/kuda-wrapped/wrapped-ux-flow.png)

After creating a wireframe based on the available data, each team member created iterations for their assigned sections. During this process, we worked closely with the UX writer to ensure clarity in how we communicated information to customers.

We held review calls twice that week and were very deliberate about testing how each section transitioned into the next.

Empty states were created for features customers didn’t use, and some sections were completely hidden where sub-features weren’t applicable.

Before launching publicly, we tested in-house to ensure the data returned was accurate and not corrupted. After a week of testing, we went [live](https://myendofyear.kuda.com/) in early January 2024.

## Outcome

The launch [sparked conversations](https://x.com/joinkuda/status/1744349824579690668) on Twitter (X), with strong positive feedback and engagement from the community.

## Takeaways

- Data and insights are valuable to users; we are exploring mini monthly reports so customers can track their finances before year-end  
- Plan end-of-year reports using the previous year’s data to avoid tight timelines  
- Think holistically: although sections were designed independently, they had to work seamlessly together  
- We gained over **1,500 new customers** in the week of go-live, the highest weekly growth in a while
