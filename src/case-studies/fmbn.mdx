The National Housing Fund is a scheme set up by the Federal Government of Nigeria to help Nigerians in paid employment gain access to affordable housing.

Employees contribute **2.5% of their monthly basic salary** to the fund, which is managed by the **Federal Mortgage Bank of Nigeria (FMBN)**.  
The FMBN uses these contributions to provide mortgages to contributors at concessionary interest rates.

---

### Benefits of the NHF Scheme

- Low-interest rates for housing loans
- Reduced tax liability, as NHF contributions are tax-deductible
- Longer loan repayment periods

---

> **Goals**
>
> - Make it easy for employers to remit employee contributions to the FMBN
> - Allow self-employed individuals or employees whose employers don’t contribute to make voluntary contributions
> - Make contributions transparent so users can view them on a mobile or web app

---

### Problem

- The process of joining and contributing to the NHF was manual and error-prone
- Contributors couldn’t edit their profile information, leading to persistent errors from manual registration
- Companies had to complete excessive paperwork after remitting employee contributions

---

### Solution

**For Employers (Web Application)**

We digitized the major processes and built a web application that allowed companies to:

- Onboard employees via batch upload using CSV or Excel files
- Automatically create NHF numbers (with default passwords) for employees who didn’t already have one
- Calculate contributions based on net base salary and automatically reflect payments on the employee’s app or web dashboard
- Manage onboarded staff and invite additional admins
