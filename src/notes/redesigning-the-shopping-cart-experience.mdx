---
title: "Redesigning The Shopping Cart Experience"
date: "2026-07-14"
excerpt: "Notes on why the cart is one of the most under-designed parts of online shopping."
---

Online shopping has become widely accepted around the world and everybody is
adopting the "shopping cart".

A cart is a basic requirement for shopping online, and it adopts one of the
simplest [mental models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mental_model) — it
literally translates to a physical cart where you put all your items before
you pay, just like in a regular store or supermarket.

Every online platform that allows shopping utilises the cart feature, even
social media apps like WhatsApp and Instagram have recently adopted an
in-app cart. Customers need a place to put the items they want to buy before
actually buying them — hence, a cart.

However, for the online shopping experience, we might not want to buy an item
right now, but we like it and would definitely want to buy it later.
